# LinkedIn Learning Video Download

For linkedin learning video download on LinkedIn, check official download or account-export options first.

For private or restricted posts, use only the account and feature that grants access. Do not bypass sign-in, privacy settings, DRM, or creator restrictions.

## How to download

1. Open the original LinkedIn post or your account export. Confirm your account may keep it; use the platform's share control if you need its link.

2. Check for Download, Save to device, or Export. If unavailable, ask the owner for the original or request your own account archive.

3. Choose an available quality and format for linkedin learning video download. Save locally, check playback, and use VLC or FFmpeg only to convert an authorized file.
