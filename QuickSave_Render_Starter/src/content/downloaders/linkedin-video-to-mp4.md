# LinkedIn Video to MP4

For linkedin video to mp4 on LinkedIn, check official download or account-export options first.

Save only media you own or have permission to use. A public link alone does not grant reuse rights; follow platform terms and applicable law.

## How to download

1. Open the original LinkedIn post or your account export. Confirm your account may keep it; use the platform's share control if you need its link.

2. Check for Download, Save to device, or Export. If unavailable, ask the owner for the original or request your own account archive.

3. Choose an available quality and format for linkedin video to mp4. Save locally, check playback, and use VLC or FFmpeg only to convert an authorized file.

## Tools to consider

Retrieve through official features. Use local editors only for authorized files; never share passwords or cookies with download sites.

| Tool | Best use |

| --- | --- |

| LinkedIn | Use media controls provided on the post or learning page, subject to account access. |

| LinkedIn data archive | Request a copy of information associated with your own account. |

| VLC or FFmpeg | Convert an authorized local media file; do not record restricted training content. |



## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use linkedin video to mp4 for someone else's post?

Only when the creator and applicable rules allow it. Credit is not permission; do not redistribute restricted media.

### Why is a download option missing?

Saving may be disabled or vary by account. Use official export or ask the owner; never bypass restrictions.

### How do I get another format or smaller file?

Use VLC or FFmpeg on an authorized file. Keep the original and check playback before sharing.
