# Download Videos with Permission

Before converting or sharing a video, confirm you created it, have permission from its owner, or have another valid right to use it.

Save only media you own or have permission to use. A public link alone does not grant reuse rights; follow platform terms and applicable law.

## How to download

1. Confirm the file's source and intended use. Ask the rights holder when ownership or permission is unclear.

2. Keep an untouched source copy. Choose a local converter and an output format suitable for the intended device.

3. Export the converted file, verify playback, and share it only within the permission you received.

## Tools to consider

These tools convert files; they do not grant rights to copy, publish, or distribute the media.

| Tool | Best use |

| --- | --- |

| VLC Media Player | Convert common audio and video files locally. |

| FFmpeg | Inspect and convert media with configurable output options. |

| HandBrake | Transcode video and select an output preset. |



## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use download videos with permission for someone else's post?

Only when the creator and applicable rules allow it. Credit is not permission; do not redistribute restricted media.

### Why is a download option missing?

Saving may be disabled or vary by account. Use official export or ask the owner; never bypass restrictions.

### How do I get another format or smaller file?

Use VLC or FFmpeg on an authorized file. Keep the original and check playback before sharing.
