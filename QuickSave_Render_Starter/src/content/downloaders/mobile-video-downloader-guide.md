# Mobile Video Downloader Guide

Use a trusted mobile editor to convert local videos for playback or sharing. Check available storage and supported export formats first.

Save only media you own or have permission to use. A public link alone does not grant reuse rights; follow platform terms and applicable law.

## How to download

1. Open a local video you own or may process. Keep the original file until the exported copy is checked.

2. Select Convert or Export in a trusted editor. Pick a format supported by the phone, tablet, or app that will play it.

3. Choose a suitable resolution to manage file size, export, and check playback before deleting the original.

## Tools to consider

Prefer on-device processing for private files. Check the editor's permissions and avoid granting access to unrelated data.
