# QuickSave SEO launch checklist

1. In Admin → Website & SEO, write a unique title and useful description.
2. After buying a domain, configure it in Render and verify HTTPS.
3. Visit `/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml` on the live domain.
4. Add the domain property to Google Search Console and submit `/sitemap.xml`.
5. Add a real social sharing image URL that you own/control.
6. Publish a privacy policy and terms page before large-scale promotion or ad monetization.
7. Write genuinely useful, original guidance for each platform. Avoid copied or thin pages.
8. Check the live homepage and guide pages with Google Search Console URL Inspection.
9. Keep external URLs current and follow the terms of each third-party platform.
10. SEO results take time; indexing and ranking are not guaranteed.

This project includes technical SEO foundations, not a guarantee of Google rankings.
